Professional menu

See, group, move, record

In the order of the Professional view's menu. Picking and sorting photos is done by AI on your Mac (Apple Vision and others).

01 · 🗂 Work Library

Every photo by date, filtered by type

  • Every photo and video in the album folder, by date, on one screen — weekdays included.
  • Filter in one click:
    PhotosVideosDocuments & receiptsScreenshotsPeopleAnimals
  • Share as PDF or Word, and optionally show them in the Mac Photos app too.
02 · 📚 Collections · 🙂 Group by face

Link-based collections — one photo, many places

  • Collections are links, so originals are never copied. The same photo can sit in a client collection and a project collection at no extra space.
  • Automatic collections (on-Mac AI): people · animals · characters & drawings · documents & receipts · screenshots (by screen size and name) · videos. Plus manual collections you build yourself.
  • Group by face: similar faces are grouped on your Mac; name a group and it becomes a 👤 person collection. Groups are an estimate — check before relying on them.
  • Face data is stored only inside the album folder and never sent anywhere.
03 · SD Card Backup

Camera card → external drive, verified

  • Copies photos and videos from the SD card to an external drive, then reads them back to verify they match.
  • When verification passes, it tells you the card is safe to erase. The app never erases the card for you.
04 · Phone Backup

Android → external drive, messenger photos included

  • Moves photos and videos from a USB-connected Android phone to an external drive (needs adb; runs only between your Mac and the phone).
  • Not just the camera roll — also photos and files received in KakaoTalk · LINE · WhatsApp · WeChat · Zalo.
05 · Drive Cleanup

Weed out duplicates and junk, sort by year and month

  • Finds duplicates and junk files, and sorts what you keep into year/month folders.
  • Every cleanup is logged, so it can be undone.
06 · Backup & Free Up Space

Back up first, verify, then free your Mac

  • Sends originals to an external drive or your own Google Drive, then frees space on your Mac.
  • Originals go to the Trash only after verification — never deleted outright.
  • If you choose Google Drive, those files are uploaded to your Google account (see the data notes below).
07 · Activity Log

Every file moved or deleted, on a verifiable record

  • Records the path, size, and MD5 of every file moved or deleted, with entries linked in a hash chain so later tampering shows.
  • Re-check any time that files are still intact. The log is stored only on this Mac.
08 · Import

From Google Photos and the Mac Photos app

  • From the Mac Photos app: your selection, an album, or a date range across the whole library.
  • From Google Photos: only the photos you pick in Google's own picker. While the app is in Google's testing phase, your account needs to be registered (hello@baryon.ai).
  • Step-by-step for each phone and computer is in the guide.
09 · Share · Writing

Export to PDF or Word; text only when you ask

  • Turn an album into a PDF or Word document for reports and sharing (converted on your Mac).
  • Claude, through your own Claude account, drafts a description only when you click. If you don't, nothing is sent.

Data & privacy

What stays on your Mac, and what leaves

Stated plainly. The full list is in the app under “ⓘ Info” → data & AI notes.

ON MACPhotos, sorting, faces

Photos live only on your Mac and the drives you connect. Type sorting, automatic collections, and face grouping are done by on-Mac AI (Apple Vision); face data stays in the album folder.

ON MACBackup, cleanup, activity log

SD card and phone backup, drive cleanup, and the activity log run only between your Mac and connected devices. File contents and names are not sent.

LEAVESGoogle Drive backup · Google Photos

Only when you choose, and only with your own Google account. Files you pick are uploaded to your Drive.

LEAVESWriting with Claude

Only when you click: small previews of a few chosen photos and a “when & where” summary (down to the neighborhood name) go to your Claude account. No originals, file names, or GPS coordinates.

LEAVESAnonymous usage counts

Baryon Labs receives only anonymous counts of feature use. See exactly what's sent, and turn it off, under “ⓘ Info”.

NOTEShow in Photos app

If iCloud Photos is on in the Mac Photos app, photos you add there may upload to your iCloud (per your Photos settings).

AI sorting and face grouping are estimates and can be wrong — check important material. It is free for work use too (companies and businesses) — only redistribution and resale are not allowed; see the license for details.

Install

Three steps

Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later)macOS 15 or laterabout 700 MBWindows 10/11 (64-bit) about 46 MB

🍎 Mac

  1. Download and open the DMG

    Click Download for Mac above, then double-click the downloaded file.

  2. Drag it to Applications

    In the window that opens, drag the “주말 사진첩” (Weekend Album) icon onto the Applications folder. On first launch, choose 🗂 Professional.

  3. The first time, “Open Anyway”

    If macOS says it can't verify the app or its developer, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, and click Open Anyway. If it says the app “is damaged and can't be opened,” paste the line below into Terminal and open the app again.

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/주말 사진첩.app"

🪟 Windows 10/11

  1. Run the installer

    Double-click WeekendAlbum-1.15.0-Setup.exe from Download for Windows above. No admin rights needed — it installs for your account only.

  2. First time: "More info → Run anyway"

    If Windows protected your PC appears, click More info, then Run anyway. It's a new app without a code-signing certificate yet, so this shows only once.

  3. Open Weekend Album and pick a view

    Open Weekend Album from the Start menu (or desktop) and choose Family or Professional. Photos go to Pictures\주말 사진첩. On Windows 10, if the window stays blank, install the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime and reopen.

  4. (Optional) Video & Android phone tools

    For video scenes/clips and phone backup, paste these lines into PowerShell to install the free tools. Face grouping, AI content sorting and Photos app sync are Mac-only.

    winget install Gyan.FFmpeg
    winget install Google.PlatformTools
