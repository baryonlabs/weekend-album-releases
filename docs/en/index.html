Beyond albums

More photos, more worries. We'll take some off your plate.

Camera cards, phones, external drives, a Mac that's full. Nothing gets deleted until it's been checked, and everything can be undone.

SD Card Backup

Pop in a card. It'll tell you when it's safe to erase.

Insert a camera or dashcam SD card and pick an external drive. The card's folders are copied as-is, then read back from the drive to make sure they match the originals. When everything checks out: “✅ This card is safe to erase.”

  • One card = one folder. Next time, the same card only adds new files
  • Hidden folders and the card's trash, too. Nothing left behind
  • Back up a stack of cards in a row with “Eject and back up next card”
Phone Backup

Downloads and chat files off your phone, space back on it

Plug in an Android phone and choose folders (Downloads, Camera, files from chat apps) to move to your external drive. Both sides are compared, and if you like, only the verified files are removed from the phone. New photos drop into your Weekend Album automatically, while the originals stay on the drive, so they barely touch your Mac's storage.

  • Files that changed after copying are never deleted
  • Just turn on “USB debugging” on the phone, once
  • On iPhone? Import by cable with Image Capture or Photos — originals kept only in iCloud are exported from the Photos app · How to import, by phone & computer →
Drive Cleanup

Messy external drive? You get a report first.

It scans the drive and shows you duplicates, junk files, empty folders, and how much space each folder takes, before anything moves. Pick what to keep and the rest goes to a holding folder; stray photos and videos get sorted into year/month folders.

  • Only files that are identical byte for byte count as duplicates
  • Every cleanup undoes with one button
  • SD card backup folders and Time Machine disks are left alone
Backup & Free Up Space

Mac full? Keep your albums, lose the bulk.

Back up your albums to an external drive, then hit Free up Mac space. Only verified originals are cleared from the Mac, and the previews stay put. Originals and videos open right up whenever the drive is connected. You can also pick big photo and video folders in your home folder and move them to the drive.

  • The drive copy is read back one more time right before deleting
  • “Bring back to Mac” whenever you want
  • Photos library, code projects, and album folders are never moved
  • Undoable until you empty the Trash
  • Back up to Google Drive and tidy up in the same go
Activity Log

What went where? Still answerable months later.

Every move and delete is recorded, down to each file. Later, tap check results again to see that everything is still on the drive, and to confirm the log itself hasn't been edited or had entries go missing.

  • Original location, new location, size, and MD5 for every file
  • Entries are chained by hashes, so tampering or gaps show up
  • The log is stored only on your Mac
Making albums

You just pick the photos

One album every weekend

Saturday and Sunday photos are grouped by weekend (“Aug, week 3”), weekday photos by month (“Sep weekdays”). Trips and events can get albums of their own, too.

From wherever they came

Pick from Google Photos, or drag in photos a friend sent you. The app reads the date taken and files each one in the right place. You can even note who took it.

A story with the when and where

Neighborhood names, dates, time of day, holidays, even words on shop signs: it pieces together how the day went, and Claude drafts it like a diary entry when you ask. Then make it yours.

Photo books and PDFs

Pick a few favorites and you've got a photo book. Bundle several weekends into a single PDF to send the family.

My Insta · AI on your Mac

AI that runs on your Mac (Apple Vision · Laya) picks best shots, hashtags, and a fitting song in seconds, without sending your photos anywhere. The song plays softly as a 30-second preview.

Fewer duplicates, more space

Finds the same photo you got twice in a chat and moves the extra to the Trash, then tidies backed-up originals to free up space on your Mac.

Install

Three steps, done

It's not from the App Store, so your Mac asks you to confirm once, the first time only.

  1. Download and open the DMG

    Click Download for Mac above, then double-click the file you downloaded.

  2. Drag it into Applications

    In the window that opens, drag the Weekend Album icon (its name shows as “주말 사진첩”) onto the Applications folder next to it.

  3. The first time, “Open Anyway”

    If macOS says it can't verify the app or its developer, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, and click Open Anyway. If it says the app “is damaged and can't be opened,” paste the line below into Terminal and open the app again.

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/주말 사진첩.app"

Photos & privacy

Your albums live on your Mac

Nothing goes to our servers

Albums are made in your Mac's Pictures folder. Baryon Labs never receives your photos, stories, or notes. Photos leave your Mac only when you choose to, like backing up to your own Google Drive.

Mostly on your Mac

Photo picking, tags, and songs are handled by AI on your Mac. Only when you ask for writing do a few chosen photos and a “when & where” summary (down to the neighborhood) go to your Claude account. Originals, file names, and GPS coordinates are not sent.

Every action is logged

The location, size, and MD5 of every file moved or deleted is kept on this Mac only, so you can check later that everything is still intact.

Anonymous usage counts only

Baryon Labs receives only anonymous counts of which features were used how often. You can see exactly what would be sent, and turn it off, under “ⓘ Info” in the app.
